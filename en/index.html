Jakujo Support

Jakujo automatically lowers your iPhone’s media volume when you leave your home Wi-Fi or arrive at an area you chose on the map. While audio plays on earbuds or similar, the mute is put on hold and applied when you take them off.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with iOS 27 or later
  • Apple’s Shortcuts app (the shortcuts change the volume)
  • For area arrival: notifications and location (While Using, Precise Location)

Getting started

In Setup, tap Add Shortcut for a trigger you want, then tap Add Shortcut in the Shortcuts app. Come back to Jakujo, tap Check It Was Added (Test Run), and follow the steps to turn on the switch.

FAQ

It doesn’t run while my iPhone is locked.
In the Shortcuts app, go to Library → Automation and make sure the switch for your trigger is on. It’s off right after you add it. After a restart, automations may not run until you unlock your iPhone once.

It says the shortcut can’t be found.
In Setup, use Add the Shortcut Again for that trigger. If a shortcut with the same name already exists, the new one gets “2” added to its name and can’t be found.

It doesn’t mute while I’m listening on earbuds.
While audio plays on earbuds or similar, the mute is put on hold. When you take them off and audio returns to the iPhone speaker, the Removing Earbuds shortcut applies it. You can see or cancel the hold on the Home tab.

Nothing happened when I arrived.
On the Arrival Area screen, check that all permissions are allowed. When you enter the area, Jakujo shows a notification saying you’ve arrived. If it doesn’t appear, save the area again.
