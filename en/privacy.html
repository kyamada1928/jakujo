Jakujo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The developer of Jakujo (“the app”) does not receive any of your information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, location, or usage data. The app makes no network connections and contains no advertising or analytics.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • Settings such as the mute volume
  • The arrival area (the center you chose on the map and its radius)
  • A history of automatic mutes (time, trigger, and the name of the audio output)

This is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your iPhone backups.

3. Location

Location is used only to center the map on your current location when you set an arrival area, and so that your iPhone can notify you when you enter that area. Your location never leaves your device. You can change the permission at any time in Settings.

4. Notifications

When you enter the arrival area, the app shows one notification. That notification is used as the trigger for an automation in the Shortcuts app.

5. Other services

  • The volume is changed by Apple’s Shortcuts app.
  • Maps are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit).
  • The shortcuts you add during setup are added to the Shortcuts app from iCloud share links.

Information in these services is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use GitHub Issues.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
